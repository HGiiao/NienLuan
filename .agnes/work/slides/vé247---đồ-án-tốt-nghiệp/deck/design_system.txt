# Design System — Vé247 PPTX

## Colors
- Primary Blue: #2563EB
- Dark Blue (navy): #0F172A
- White: #FFFFFF
- Light Gray: #F1F5F9
- Accent Yellow: #F59E0B
- Gradient: #2563EB → #1D4ED8
- Table Header: #2563EB white text
- Table Rows: alternating white / #F1F5F9
- Text Primary: #0F172A
- Text Secondary: #64748B
- Text Light (on dark bg): #FFFFFF
- Border: #E2E8F0

## Typography
- Title font: Arial Black / Inter Bold
- Body font: Inter / Arial
- Slide title: 32px bold
- Body: 14px
- Table: 11px
- Small captions: 10px

## Layout Rules
- Slide size: 1280x720 (16:9)
- Each slide must have: title (top-left, with accent underline), content filling most of the slide
- NO empty slides — every slide must be information-dense
- Use tables, columns, bullet points, diagrams to fill space
- Footer: slide number

## Slide Types
1. **Cover** (slides 1, 22, 23): dark bg #0F172A, white text, centered
2. **Content** (rest): white bg, left-aligned title with accent underline
3. **Diagram**: include local image from source
4. **Table**: 2-3 columns, header blue, alternating rows
5. **Grid of cards**: for feature highlights
6. **Flow/Process**: horizontal steps with arrows

## Visual Elements
- Arrows: CHEVRON shapes
- Boxes: rounded rectangles
- Icons: use Unicode/emoji where appropriate
- Tables: full-width with clear headers
